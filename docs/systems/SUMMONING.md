# Summoning – concept

Source: design doc, "Summoning System" (Normal, Faction, Fragment, Event, Guardian Summon; Summon Scrolls, Gems,
event currency, special tickets; pity) and "Building System" (Summoning Gate: on later upgrades it expands recruitment
to specialized heroes). Numbers are first-pass balancing.

| Banner | Opens with | Cost (1×) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal Summon | Summoning Gate 1 | 1 Summon Scroll or 300 Gems | – |
| Faction Summon | Summoning Gate 2 | 2 Summon Scrolls or 500 Gems | 70 % of pulls from the chosen faction |
| Fragment Summon | Summoning Gate 3 | 2 Summon Scrolls or 500 Gems | 70 % of pulls from the chosen Fragment Aspect |

- Grade rates are the same on every banner (Common 50 %, Rare 38 %, Epic 10.5 %, Legendary 1.5 %); ten pulls with
  Gems cost 10 % less.
- Pity per banner: an Epic or better at the latest every 10 pulls, a Legendary at the latest every 50.
- A focused pull takes a hero of the rolled grade from the focus; if the focus has none of that grade, the next lower
  grade of the focus is used.
- Not yet: Event Summon (needs Event Portals) and Guardian Summon (needs Guardian-linked heroes).
