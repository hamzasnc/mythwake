# Shattered Realms – concept

Source: design doc, "Shattered Realm System" (cracks and portals into broken dimensions; a limited dungeon that opens
twice a day or with a key for the day, the key from a shop or a daily reward; story missions, events, special
dungeons, high-level challenges, rare material farming, Corrupted Guardian encounters; each realm has its own rules,
visuals and enemies; the four example realms), "Building System" (Portal Chamber: Shattered Realm access), "Enemy
System" (Riftborn: creatures from the Shattered Realms; Corrupted Guardians). Numbers are first-pass balancing.

## Realms

The doc's four examples, each a high-level challenge with 60 difficulty levels. Enemies are Riftborn escorting a
Corrupted Guardian–class boss.

| Realm | Rule (doc) | How the rules do it | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realm of Ash | Fire damage is stronger, healing is weaker | Enemies +25 % damage and may burn, healing −40 % | Dragon Echo of the Sky |
| Realm of Stillness | Enemies slow the team and delay skills | Enemy hits may slow and push back actions, ultimates charge 25 % slower | Corrupted Guardian of the Deep |
| Realm of Reflection | Enemies copy buffs and reflect damage | Enemy hits may steal a buff; enemies hit back when struck | False Guardian Construct |
| Realm of Hunger | Enemies regenerate and become stronger over time | Enemies regenerate 2 % max HP per second, +6 % damage every 10 s (up to +50 %) | Fragment Devourer |

## Entries and keys

- **Two free entries per day** for all realms together (doc: "opens twice a day"), reset at the daily reset (04:00 UTC),
  unused entries do not stack.
- **Shattered Realm Keys** (doc: "a key … from a shop or daily reward system") open one more entry. Sources: the last
  daily quest chest (100 points) and the Hunt Shop (weekly limit 2).
- Only a won fight uses an entry (free entries first, then a key).
- Clearing level N opens N+1; first clears pay extra. Sweep repeats the highest cleared level for one entry.

## Rewards (rare material farming)

Per clear: Guardian Sigils, Fragment Dust, Relic materials and Gold, growing with the level. First clears add Gems
and more Guardian Sigils. Guardian Sigils are kept for Guardian Awakening (doc), which comes later.

## Unlock

Portal Chamber level 5 (doc: the Portal Chamber unlocks Shattered Realm access). The Shattered Realms pin on the world
map opens them.
