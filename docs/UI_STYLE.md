# UI style rules (after studying AFK Arena and 7DS Idle Adventure)

Reference screens studied on 26.10.2026: AFK Arena (campaign, stage popup, hero detail, victory, monthly card – Game UI
Database), The Seven Deadly Sins: IDLE (main stage, world map, tavern – App Store screenshots). What both do, and what
Mythwake does from now on:

1. **World first.** The campaign hub and hero detail are full-screen scenes. UI floats on the edges: a top bar, side
   columns of round icon buttons with a small outlined label, one big action at the bottom. No stacked panels over the
   world.
2. **Warm materials.** Content sits on light parchment (cream with a brown wood / gold frame) with dark brown text;
   chrome (top bar, nav dock) is dark wood with bronze. No cold navy blocks.
3. **One glowing primary action** per screen in teal/cyan ("Kampf", "Level Up", "Einsammeln"); secondary actions in
   wood; purchases show their cost in a small dark pill.
4. **Icons over words.** Menu icons share one style (bronze/sepia relief) with a short label; only items and
   currencies are in full colour. Numbers go next to icons, not in sentences.
5. **Readable text.** Text on art is white with a dark outline; text on parchment is dark brown. Section titles sit on a
   thin dark ribbon.
6. **Items always in square slots** with the grade frame and the amount at the bottom right.
7. **Results are full-screen moments:** dark illustrated backdrop, big VICTORY/DEFEAT banner, rewards in slots,
   "tap to continue".
8. **Hero detail is a stage:** the hero big in the middle, grade/name/Aspect on top, one row of stats with icons, the
   level-up button below, everything else further down.
